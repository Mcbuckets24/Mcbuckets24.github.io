I try to bring one of my cameras everywhere I go. I like taking pictures of my friends and everything around me. I'm not great at it, but I have a lot of fun with it. I primarily use my Sony DCR-TRV240 HI-8 Camcorder for video recording, but I also have Kodak EasyShare C310 that I like to take some photos with. I occasionally use my iPhone for photos as well, but I prefer the older look and feel of my other cameras.
